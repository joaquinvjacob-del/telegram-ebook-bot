# The Free Library Method — Audiobook Edition

### Free Classic Audiobooks, Chapter by Chapter, Straight to Your Phone

## Introduction

Welcome — you now have everything you need to listen to almost any classic book, read aloud by a real human voice, completely free.

In the next few minutes, you're going to:
- Activate your audiobook access with the code you received
- Search for your first audiobook
- Get your first chapter playing

**One important thing up front:** audiobooks are a *separate* feature from ebook text downloads. If you've already bought the ebook guide, this is an add-on — it uses its own access code and works alongside what you already have, not instead of it. If this is the only thing you bought, that's fine too — everything below works on its own.

### What this actually is

Behind the scenes, this gives you access to a private tool connected to **LibriVox** — a huge library of classic books read aloud by volunteers, completely free and legal. These are real human narrators, not robotic text-to-speech. You get each chapter as its own audio file, delivered directly in the chat, playable instantly with a normal play/pause bar — just like any voice message.

### What this is *not*

To be completely clear: every audiobook here is a **public-domain recording** of a public-domain book — the copyright expired long ago, and LibriVox volunteers recorded it for free, for anyone to use. This is not piracy, and it's not a gray area. You're not "getting away with" anything.

Let's get you listening.

## Part 1 — Installing Telegram

*(Already have Telegram set up and the bot open from the ebook guide? Skip straight to Part 2.)*

Your access runs inside an app called **Telegram** — a free, extremely popular messaging app (similar to WhatsApp), available on every phone, tablet, and computer.

### On iPhone / iPad
1. Open the **App Store**.
2. Tap the search icon and type **Telegram**.
3. Tap **Get**, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password.
4. Once it's installed, tap **Open**.

### On Android
1. Open the **Play Store**.
2. Search for **Telegram**.
3. Tap **Install**.
4. Once it's installed, tap **Open**.

### On a computer (Windows / Mac)
1. Go to **telegram.org** in your browser.
2. Click **Get Telegram**, and download the version for your operating system.
3. Install it like you would any other program, then open it.

*(You can also just use Telegram directly in your browser at web.telegram.org — no installation needed.)*

### Creating your account (first-time Telegram users only)

When you open Telegram for the first time, it will ask for your phone number to create your account. This is completely normal — it's how Telegram verifies you're a real person. It will text you a short verification code; type that code in, and you're in.

## Part 2 — Activating Your Audiobook Access

1. Inside Telegram, find the same bot you used for ebooks (or search for it fresh if this is your first time — the bot name is in your purchase confirmation).
2. Tap **Start** if you haven't already.
3. Send this exact message:

```
/unlock AUDIO2020
```

4. You should immediately get a confirmation:

```
✅ Audiobooks unlocked! Try /audiobook <title>.
```

That's it — audiobooks are now active on your account.

### If your code doesn't work

- **This is a different code from the ebook one.** If you already unlocked ebooks, that code won't work here — audiobooks need this separate one.
- **Double-check for typos.** The code doesn't care about uppercase or lowercase, but every character needs to be exact — copy-paste it instead of retyping it.
- **Make sure there's a space** between `/unlock` and the code.
- **"This code has already been used on another account"** — message us on Instagram (see the Support section) and we'll sort it out immediately.

## Part 3 — How to Use the Bot for Audiobooks

**Read this part carefully — it's the one place audiobooks work differently from ebooks.**

For ebooks, you just type a title with no command. For audiobooks, you have to use the `/audiobook` command every time — typing a title alone will search ebooks instead, not audiobooks.

### Searching for an audiobook

```
/audiobook Dracula
```

Send it, and within a couple of seconds you'll get a numbered list of matching audiobooks.

**💡 Tip:** LibriVox's search only matches the *beginning* of a title, and sometimes drops a leading "The"/"A"/"An" from how a book is actually listed. If a search comes up empty, try:
- Dropping "The"/"A"/"An" from the start of the title (e.g. `/audiobook Adventures of Sherlock Holmes` instead of `/audiobook The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes`)
- Searching just the author's last name (e.g. `/audiobook Stoker`)

### Picking your audiobook

Tap the number next to the title you want, same as with ebooks.

### Getting your chapters

You'll see a numbered list of chapters with their length (e.g. `1. Chapter I (12:34)`). Tap any chapter number and it arrives right in the chat as a playable audio file — tap it and it plays immediately, with a normal play/pause bar and scrub control, exactly like a voice message.

If there are more chapters than fit on one screen, use the navigation arrows (⏮ ◀️ ▶️ ⏭) to page through them.

**📦 Want the whole book at once?** Look for the **"Download all (ZIP)"** button below the chapter list — one tap downloads every chapter bundled together in a single file.

### Quick access to popular audiobooks

If you don't have a specific title in mind, send:

```
/popularaudio
```

This instantly shows you 10 of the most-requested classic audiobooks — no searching required.

### Starting fresh

`/reset` cleans up the chat and shows the welcome message again. It never affects your access — it's purely a "tidy up the chat" button.

## Part 4 — Listening on Your Device

Good news: unlike ebooks, there's no Kindle-style format headache here. Every chapter is a standard MP3 — it plays anywhere, on anything.

### The easiest way: just listen inside Telegram

You don't have to do anything extra. Tap a chapter, and it plays right there in the chat with a play/pause bar you can scrub back and forth on — most people never need to leave Telegram at all.

### Listening without opening Telegram (offline, or in another app)

1. Tap the chapter's audio message.
2. Tap the **download/save icon** to save it to your phone.
3. Open it from your **Files** app (iPhone) or **Downloads** folder (Android) — or import it into any music/podcast-style player you prefer.

### Listening in the car or with headphones

No special setup needed — connect your headphones or your car's Bluetooth the same way you always do, then just play the chapter from inside Telegram like you would any audio message. It'll play through your headphones or car speakers automatically.

### Getting every chapter onto your computer at once

1. Tap the **"Download all (ZIP)"** button under any chapter list.
2. Save the ZIP file to your computer.
3. Extract it (right-click → **Extract All** on Windows, or double-click on Mac) — you'll get every chapter as a separate MP3 file, ready to drag into iTunes, a music folder, or any player you like.
